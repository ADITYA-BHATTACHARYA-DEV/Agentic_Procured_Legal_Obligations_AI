    How it works

    From upload to tracked, in four steps.

    01

    Upload

    Drop a PDF or an image. Scanned pages are read with OCR.

    02

    Read and index

    The text is split, embedded and stored on your machine so answers can cite pages.

    03

    Extract and assign

    Obligations and renewal windows are pulled out, dated in code and routed to owners.

    04

    Track and remind

    Tasks, reminders at 30, 7 and 1 days, and a calendar file keep nothing from slipping.
